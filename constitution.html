Constitution of the Republic of Rooftopia

Adopted: April 20, 2026 | Ratified by the Rooftop Assembly

We, the citizens of Rooftopia, standing upon our elevated soil, do hereby establish this Constitution to secure the blessings of rooftop liberty, promote the cultivation of plants and community, and ensure that every sunrise brings dignity to our small but sovereign nation.

Article I: Territorial Sovereignty

Section 1: The territory of Rooftopia includes the entire rooftop surface at [Your Address], including all planters, solar panels, seating areas, and the airspace up to 3 meters above the highest point.

Section 2: The boundaries shall be marked by the edge of the roof. Any expansion (new planters, furniture) constitutes territorial growth.

Section 3: The sky above and the ceiling below are recognized as natural borders.

Article II: Citizenship

Section 1: Citizenship is granted to humans, plants, and companion animals recognized by the Supreme Guardian.

Section 2: Naturalization requires: (a) Submission of application, (b) Oath of Rooftop Loyalty, (c) Participation in one sunset ceremony.

Section 3: Citizens have the right to name one plant, attend government meetings, and propose decrees.

Article III: The Supreme Guardian

Section 1: The Supreme Guardian (founder) holds executive authority to issue decrees, manage diplomacy, and protect the realm.

Section 2: Decrees must be posted publicly (on this website) and remain in effect unless revoked.

Section 3: The Guardian may appoint ministers for specific domains (Sunlight, Watering, Bird Relations).

Article IV: The Rooftop Assembly

Section 1: Any citizen may call a meeting (in person or virtual) on the first Saturday of each month.

Section 2: Assembly resolutions are advisory to the Guardian but carry strong moral weight.

Section 3: Plants may be represented by their caretaker.

Article V: Laws of the Land

5.1 Environmental Protection

No stepping on moss zones. All plants must be watered according to schedule. Solar panels shall not be shaded.

5.2 Cultural Observances

Every citizen must wave at passing clouds. Friday afternoons are for tea diplomacy.

5.3 Immigration

Visitors must announce themselves. Diplomatic envoys are welcome with prior notice.

Article VI: Passports & National Identity

Section 1: The Rooftopia Passport is a ceremonial document issued to citizens upon approval.

Section 2: Passports contain the citizen's name, citizen number, and issue date.

Section 3: Passports are not valid for international travel but serve as proof of Rooftopian citizenship.

Article VII: Amendments

This Constitution may be amended by a decree from the Supreme Guardian after 7 days of public notice. Amendments will be posted on this website.

So declared this 20th day of April, 2026,

Supreme Guardian [Your Name]
Founder of the Republic of Rooftopia

Witnessed by the first tomato plant, "Sir Reddington"
