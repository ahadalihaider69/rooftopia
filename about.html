About Rooftopia

Learn about our nation, provinces, and values

🏛️ Our Nation

Rooftopia is a model micronation founded on April 20, 2026. Located 15 meters above street level, we are a sovereign nation dedicated to community, creativity, and democratic values. Our motto, "Above the Ordinary," reflects our commitment to excellence and innovation.

🏙️ Our Provinces

🏙️ PR1 Province

The urban hub of Rooftopia. Home to diplomatic centers, rooftop cafes, and the Supreme Guardian's office.

🌾 PR2 Province

The agricultural heartland. Known for community gardens, herb cultivation, and traditional values.

🏜️ PR3 Province

The mystic lands. Famous for solar energy zones, desert landscapes, and ancient rooftop secrets.

📜 Our Values

  • Democracy - Every citizen's voice matters
  • Community - We grow together
  • Innovation - Always looking above the ordinary
  • Sustainability - Green energy and plant cultivation
